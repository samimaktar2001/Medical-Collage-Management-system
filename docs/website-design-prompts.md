# Built-in Image Gen prompts

All three calls received the same current-site screenshot path recorded in website-design-options.md. No external design connector was used for generation.

## First displayed concept

Use case: ui-mockup. Create realistic, production-quality UI design with clear hierarchy, strong typography, purposeful spacing. Design ONE desktop institutional medical college homepage, target dimensions 1440 x 1200. Current date 28 September 2026. Attached screenshot is the current Medora site: retain its fictional Medora Medical College identity and navy #102a43, teal #0f766e design family, but REPLACE the generic startup hero/card catalogue with a formal, content-rich Indian medical college information service. This is an original design, never government branding. Show small persistent 'DEMONSTRATION · Synthetic institution and content' line. No government seals, national emblems, claimed affiliation, accreditations, fake awards, inflated statistics, live seat numbers or doctor stock photography. Do not put options/numbers or mockup explanation outside the UI. Full-bleed page surface, no device or browser frame.
Use exactly two readable fonts at most: Manrope heading and DM Sans body; body16px, strong hierarchy. Warm white base, thin dividers, restrained square corners, no nested cards, no excessive shadows, no huge marketing slogans. Primary use: prospective applicant finds notice and admissions guidance quickly. Group service links instead of marketing inventory. Provide utility links Skip to content / Accessibility / Contact, institution masthead with honest 'Academic and institutional information' descriptor and search, Portal login, complete compact main navigation: Home, About, Academics, Admissions, Departments, Hospital, Research, Notices, Student Services, Contact. Main area must prominently include Notice Board dated synthetic examples 28 Sep 2026 reporting-document checklist, 25 Sep 2026 academic orientation schedule, 22 Sep 2026 library induction. Notice categories and all-notices link. Adjacent admission summary three distinct lines 'Admission stage: Scheduled (demo)', 'Local form window: Not yet open', 'Seat vacancy: Not yet published'; primary CTA 'View admission guidance', NO apply button implying open. Short secondary row Academic calendar / Forms & downloads / Student support. Footer visibly structured with institution, useful links, policies and demo content owner, no made-up address/phone. A modest banner may use original architectural illustration ONLY captioned 'Illustrative campus concept — not a real facility'; avoid photographic facility claims. Keep content readable and uncluttered, modest banner and notices visible early. Render one coherent usable screen, no cards just to advertise features. Preserve these requirements in each direction.
Direction name: Institutional bulletin. Visual strategy: dignified full-width navy navigation under generous white masthead; a narrow pale-blue editorial introduction with no imagery and a concise 'Academic information & admissions' heading. Then a large 2:1 layout with highly readable notice rows left, lightly tinted admissions panel right. No oversized hero. Below use restrained typographic programme/department links and complete footer. Feel established, precise, calm and service-oriented.
